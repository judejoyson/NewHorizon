About Us

Outstanding Care from Experienced Staff

New Horizon Group Homes is a family-run provider of residential care in Seffner, Florida, serving adults with developmental and physical disabilities. Our homes are licensed by the Florida Agency for Persons with Disabilities (APD) and managed by professionals with nursing and social work backgrounds.

We believe every resident deserves dignity, independence, and a true sense of belonging. From home-cooked meals in bright, modern kitchens to quiet afternoons on the porch under Florida oaks, daily life at New Horizon is built around comfort, safety, and community.

  • Florida APD-licensed residential facilities
  • Staff trained in nursing and social work
  • Personalized care plans for every resident
  • Safe, clean, fully furnished private bedrooms
  • Peaceful rural setting with domestic animals and birds
